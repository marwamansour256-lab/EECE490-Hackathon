Date: 17 October 2026
Scope: README.md, src/pitch_visuals.py, notebooks 02/03/04.
Notebook cell references are zero-based. Original scripts/notebooks were preserved.

AUTHORITATIVE FRAMING
The hackathon PDF explicitly describes real transaction data with replacement
identifiers. SYN- transaction IDs do not demonstrate synthetic transactions.
This corrects the original assertions rather than substituting another inferred
generation story. Proceed under the brief's stated provenance with documented
assumptions. Missing operational fields are a deployment data contract, not a
prerequisite for completing the hackathon.

1. FINANCIAL SCENARIOS ARE NOT FORECASTS; ELASTICITY IS NOT IDENTIFIED
References: pitch_visuals.py lines 375-419; original README lines 82-104.
All 135 branch-product combinations have one list price over Jan-Jun. Price is
confounded with branch; ordinary list-price elasticity is not identified. Small
nonrandomized discounts do not establish that a permanent increase retains volume.
The approximately $65k annual total doubles six months, assumes unchanged demand,
assumes 10% add-on acceptance, and treats recorded cost differences and discounts
as recoverable profit. These are headroom scenarios, not realized savings, annual
forecasts, net profit or ROI. Show demand/cost/acceptance sensitivity and test
incremental gross profit in a controlled pilot. Conditional break-even formulas
do not estimate how customers actually respond. Account for interactions,
substitution, capacity and implementation costs when combining actions.

2. ACQUISITION IS CONFUSED WITH FIRST APPEARANCE
References: pitch_visuals.py lines 199-231; notebook 04 cells 25-26 and 30.
The observation window starts January 1. First observed is not newly acquired;
January includes existing customers. Falling first-seen counts can occur as a
stable customer pool becomes observed. Withdraw acquisition-collapse claims.
Use first-seen terminology unless registration or earlier history exists.
The 91% statistic is overlap between two selected two-month windows, not a
generally calibrated retention probability.

3. IN-SAMPLE MODEL FIT IS PRESENTED AS PREDICTIVE/CAUSAL EVIDENCE
References: pitch_visuals.py lines 109-120 and 140.
The 93% R-squared fits and evaluates the same 181 days. Event dates are included
in the training sample, and trend can absorb seasonal variation. Small residual
weather correlations do not prove no external response, price insensitivity or
habit. Use rolling-origin held-out predictions, calendar/lag baselines, external
feature ablation and uncertainty. Jointly adjusted retrospective associations
still need separate causal identification. Historical realized future weather is
not a deployable forecast-time input; label retrospective oracle comparisons.

4. EXACT-ROW DELETION IS AN UNRESOLVED LINE-SEMANTICS ASSUMPTION
References: pitch_visuals.py line 72; notebooks 02/03/04 cell 2.
No unique line ID distinguishes duplicate imports from repeated identical items
in one order. There are 132 repeated excess rows affecting 132 orders.
Raw: 51,177 rows; 54,087 units; 33,887 orders; revenue $305,925.14;
gross profit $179,430.49.
After exact deduplication: 51,045 rows; 53,955 units; 33,887 orders;
revenue $305,183.71; gross profit $178,994.28.
Deletion removes $741.43 revenue and $436.21 gross profit. Order counts are
unaffected. Preserve raw data and present sensitivity pending deployed data
contract confirmation. Unidentified customers are not necessarily walk-ins.

5. HOLIDAY BANDS AND MULTIPLE COMPARISONS OVERSTATE UNUSUAL EVENTS
References: notebook 03 cells 4, 6, 9-11 and 21.
The symmetric percentage band +/- (exp(1.96*sd)-1) is not the asymmetric band
implied by symmetric log residuals: exp(-1.96*sd)-1 to exp(1.96*sd)-1. The method
also omits prediction leverage, parameter uncertainty and residual dependence.
There are 17 holidays across four series plus type/product/behavior comparisons.
Harbour's five exceptions are exploratory, not multiplicity-corrected findings.
Use held-out residual bands or blocked resampling, predefined test families and
false-discovery control. One occurrence per holiday cannot establish repeatable
annual effects. Public calendar categories do not identify customer religion.

6. WAR DATES CONFLICT; CAUSAL INTERPRETATION IS UNSUPPORTED
References: notebook 02 cells 0, 2, 9, 11-12 and 21;
pitch_visuals.py lines 93-102.
Notebook: March 1-April 15, with April 16 boundary. Pitch: March 2-April 16,
with April 17 truce. The new analysis uses UN/UNHCR-sourced escalation on
March 2 and temporary ceasefire effective April 17 local time. See
reports/external_evidence.json. Continuing hostilities mean no clean postwar
period; maintain sensitivity to dates and exposure definitions.
The pre-war-only trend fit is preferable to full-sample fitting for a descriptive
counterfactual, but extrapolates about two months without intervals, alternative
trends, unaffected controls or geographic exposure. Exponentiating predicted log
orders also needs attention to mean retransformation bias. Near-zero deviation
does not demonstrate no war effect. A Garden gap that grows later does not rule
out lagged effects. Avoid war-resilience claims from these comparisons.

7. BRANCH ALIASES DO NOT ESTABLISH GEOGRAPHY OR EXTERNAL EXPOSURE
References: pitch_visuals.py lines 34-37; notebook 02 cell 21.
Harbour/Garden/Uptown do not establish actual regions or branch coordinates.
Beirut weather is a proxy scenario until mapping is established. Regional conflict
exposure cannot be assigned from these names. Operational deployment requires
locations, hours/closures, access disruption and location-specific exposure.

8. UNADJUSTED PROMOTION AVERAGES ARE NOT INCREMENTAL PROFIT
References: pitch_visuals.py lines 283-330.
Eight-day promo windows and fourteen-day comparison windows have different weekday
composition; trend, Ramadan, war and holidays overlap. Plotting other branches is
not an adjusted difference-in-differences estimate. Use branch-category-day panels,
weekday/trend adjustment, pretrend checks, uncertainty and basket-wide profit.
Advertising exposure is unknown. Similar raw units/day is a descriptive result;
price cuts buying no volume is not established.

9. CHURN HEURISTIC CAN EXCLUDE PREVIOUSLY VALUABLE LAPSED CUSTOMERS
References: notebook 04 cells 2, 28-30.
Whole-period regularity includes the eventual lapse. Previously frequent customers
can become Occasional and leave the target pool. Median completed visit gaps omit
the current censored gap. Freeze segments at historical cutoffs, evaluate later
return/nonreturn, and calibrate outreach on incremental profit. The flagged group's
historical $12k spend is not forecast loss or recoverable value. Claims that they
are the cheapest customers to recover require response/cost evidence.

10. FAVORITE-SHARE COMPARISONS DO NOT RULE OUT PERSONALIZATION
References: pitch_visuals.py lines 333-370.
One favorite-product statistic versus one random-menu simulation is not a test
of recommendation effectiveness. Context, sparse histories and uncertainty matter.
Compare contextual suggestions against a global popular-item baseline on held-out
data and then in a randomized conversion/profit pilot.

11. JANUARY-JUNE IS NOT ANNUAL SEASONALITY
Six months contain one winter-to-summer transition and cannot separate repeated
seasonality from trend and coincident events. Describe observed monthly differences,
test identifiable weather variation, and do not forecast autumn or double six-month
benefits as an established annual return.

RECOMMENDED HACKATHON APPROACH
Build and validate a branch/category demand forecast that tests external context
against calendar and lag baselines. Retain external features only with credible
validation; allow an honest no-improvement result. Convert forecasts to decisions
using explicit understock/overstock or promotion economics, with uncertainty and
abstention. Without availability, waste and operating-cost records, profit uplift
is a simulation requiring a pilot, not measured business value. See
reports/deep_analysis_report.pdf, src/external_analysis.py and src/demand_model.py.

BEFORE LIVE DEPLOYMENT (NOT A BLOCKER FOR HACKATHON ANALYSIS)
Establish branch geography/timezones, line semantics, opening/closure calendars,
availability and stockouts, waste/ingredient and labor costs, promotion exposure,
customer registration dates, and archived information available at decision time.
