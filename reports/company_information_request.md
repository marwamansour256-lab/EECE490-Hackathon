# Information request for Stories (draft)

*Draft prepared by Omar Al-Aghbari (external evidence, teammate 1), 2026-10-17. To be sent to the Stories team or the hackathon organizers by the team. Not yet sent.*

We are building a preparation planner from Stories' January–June 2026 transactions. The questions below are what we need to check which outside events affected which branch, and whether our profit figures match reality. Answers to section A matter most. Partial answers are useful; "we don't know" is a fine answer too.

## A. Branch locations and hours (most important)

1. **Where is each branch?** For Harbour, Garden and Uptown: the town or neighborhood, or ideally an address or map pin.
2. **Opening hours** for each branch, and whether they changed between January and June 2026 (for example during Ramadan or the March–April war period).
3. **Closures:** any days or part-days when a branch was closed or had limited service (holidays, security situation, power cuts, road closures, renovations). Dates and times if possible.
4. **Time zone of the timestamps:** are the transaction times Beirut local time, and do they follow daylight saving time (clocks changed on 29 March 2026)?

## B. Costs and prices

5. **What is "Unit Cost (USD)"?** Is it a fixed standard recipe cost, or the actual cost paid each month? Was it updated after March 2026, when food and fuel prices in Lebanon rose sharply?
6. If actual costs changed, **monthly ingredient cost per product** (or per main ingredient) for January–June 2026.
7. **Garden's costs** are about 43% higher than Harbour's for every product. Is that real (different supplier, smaller deliveries, rent included), or a difference in how costs are recorded?
8. Were **menu prices** changed at any point in 2026, or planned to change?

## C. Discounts and promotions

9. **How is the 10% discount granted?** (staff, loyalty, coupon, manager's discretion, partner card?)
10. Was the **15% Cold Coffee and Desserts promotion at Harbour** (12–19 March and 12–19 May) advertised? Where and how?
11. Any **other promotions, events or campaigns** in January–June 2026 that aren't visible as discounts (social media, catering, events at the branch)?

## D. Operations

12. **Stockouts:** do you record when an item sold out? If so, which items and when.
13. **Preparation and waste:** how many units of fresh items (sandwiches, pastries, desserts) are prepared each day, and how much is thrown away?
14. **Second batch:** can a branch prepare more items in the afternoon? How long does it take and what does it cost in staff time?
15. **What does one row of the data mean?** Some rows are exact duplicates (same order, product, time and price). Are those two separate items or a double record?

## E. Customers

16. **Customer ID:** is it a loyalty card, phone number or account? When did the program start, and can a customer have more than one ID? (We see new IDs drop from about 2,000 in January to 20 in June.)

---

*How the answers will be used:* branch locations and hours decide which events and weather can be linked to each branch; costs decide every profit figure; stockouts and preparation decide whether the planner's savings are real. Until we have them, the analysis treats branch exposure as "unknown" and all profit figures as scenarios.
